# Planet Profile: Kepler-Vex

Kepler-Vex is the third planet orbiting the K-type star Vex. It is an ice world with a mean surface temperature of -142 °C and a surface gravity of 0.71 g. One local day (a "sol") lasts 31.4 hours.

## Structure
The planet is covered by an ice shell estimated at 18–24 km thick. Gravity and magnetometer data from the ODY-02 Halcyon orbital survey strongly suggest a liquid subsurface ocean beneath the shell. The atmosphere is thin (0.08 bar), composed mostly of nitrogen with traces of methane.

## Surface sites
- **Halvorsen Basin**: a smooth impact basin near the equator, chosen as the landing site for Meridian Station because the ice there is thought to be thinnest (approx. 18 km).
- **Corvane Ridges**: fractured terrain 140 km north of the basin, possible cryovolcanic origin.

## Hazards
Extreme cold causes brittleness in carbide drill bits below -130 °C. Surface winds rarely exceed 12 m/s. Radiation dose is low (0.3 Sv/year) due to distance from the star.
