# RD-440 Regolith and Ice Drill: Operations Manual (v2, supersedes v1)

Issued after the BH-2 bit fracture on Kepler-Vex (Sol 43).

## Specifications
- Maximum drilling depth: **25 m in ice colder than -130 °C**; 40 m otherwise.
- Bit type: CB-9X reinforced carbide (CB-9 bits are withdrawn).
- Maximum rotation: 150 rpm
- Rated operating temperature: down to -160 °C

## Warning codes
- **V-11 (vibration):** FULL STOP. Retract the bit and inspect before resuming. (Changed from v1.)
- **V-20 (overheat):** stop drilling for 10 minutes.
