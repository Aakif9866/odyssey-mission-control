# Science Report: Anomaly ANM-0042

## Observation
A periodic seismic signal at 0.37 Hz was detected beneath Halvorsen Basin, first on Sol 42 with a period of 6.2 hours, shortening to 5.9 hours by Sol 48. The signal was recorded independently by seismometers S1, S2 and S4 (S3 was offline for maintenance), which rules out a single-instrument artifact.

## Source estimate
Triangulation places the source at roughly 19 km depth, close to the expected ice–ocean boundary.

## Hypotheses (ranked)
1. **Tidal flexing** of the ice shell (most likely; consistent with orbital period harmonics).
2. **Subsurface ocean currents** interacting with the shell base.
3. **Cryovolcanic activity** linked to the Corvane Ridges (least likely; the source is 140 km away).

Confidence: moderate. The period change is not yet explained by any hypothesis.

## Recommendations
- Deploy four additional seismometers in a triangle with 12 km spacing (requires Director approval as a new hardware deployment).
- Do not resume drilling beyond 25 m until the RD-440 v2 limits are confirmed safe.
