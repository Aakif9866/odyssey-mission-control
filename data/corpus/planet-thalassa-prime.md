# Planet Profile: Thalassa Prime

Thalassa Prime is the largest moon of the gas giant Aurelia. It hosts a global liquid-water ocean confirmed in 2231 by the probe Siren.

## Structure
The ice crust is 3–5 km thick at the poles but thins to approximately 0.9 km at the **Tethys Rift**, a 600 km long equatorial fracture zone. Surface gravity is 0.22 g. Surface temperature averages -170 °C.

## Radiation
Because Thalassa Prime orbits inside Aurelia's magnetosphere, the surface radiation dose is 5.4 Sv/year, far above the HED crew limit. Crewed EVAs on the surface are limited to a maximum of 4 hours. Any crewed landing requires a radiation waiver approved by the Director of Exploration.

## Scientific interest
Plumes of water vapour are periodically observed venting from the Tethys Rift, making it the most accessible point to sample the ocean directly.
