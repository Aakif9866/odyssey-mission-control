# Mission Brief: ODY-05 Deepwater

**Destination:** Thalassa Prime, Tethys Rift
**Planned launch:** 2242-06-01
**Crew:** 4 plus the cryobot NAUTILUS-2 | **Status:** Planning
**Risk class:** A (highest)

## Objectives
1. Land near the Tethys Rift where the ice is approximately 0.9 km thick.
2. Deploy NAUTILUS-2, a nuclear-heated cryobot that melts through ice at 2.5 m per hour, to reach the ocean (estimated transit time: about 15 days).
3. Collect ocean water samples and return them to the surface lander.

## Open issues
- The mission cannot proceed until the Director of Exploration, Halima Sato, approves a radiation waiver (surface dose 5.4 Sv/year).
- Crew surface EVAs are capped at 4 hours.

## Crew
Commander Amara Lindqvist; Dr. Felix Osei (oceanographer); Engineer Rhea Castellanos; Dr. Mei-Lin Zhou (radiation physician).
