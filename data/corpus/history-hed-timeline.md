# Helios Exploration Directorate (HED): Historical Timeline

- **2189**: HED founded.
- **2203**: ODY-01 Firstlight, the first crewed HED mission (Cinder orbit).
- **2217**: ODY-02 Halcyon orbital survey of Kepler-Vex discovers evidence of a subsurface ocean.
- **2231**: Probe Siren confirms a global ocean on Thalassa Prime.
- **2236**: Funding freeze delays ODY-03 Meridian.
- **2239**: ODY-04 Ember lands on Cinder; Kiln-1 lost to a lava tube collapse.
- **2240**: ODY-03 Meridian launches (March) and arrives at Kepler-Vex (November).
- **2241**: Halima Sato serves as Director of Exploration; ODY-05 Deepwater enters planning.
