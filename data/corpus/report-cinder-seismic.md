# Science Report: Cinder Seismic Observations (Kiln-2)

Lander Kiln-2 records frequent seismic events up to magnitude 4.1. Events cluster in a regular cycle of about 6 hours, driven by tidal heating from the star. The dominant frequency is 1.2 Hz.

Kiln-1 was lost on 2239-08-19 when a roof section of the Ashfall Tubes collapsed beneath it. Kiln-2 has been operating since 2239-09-02 and avoids tube regions mapped by ground-penetrating radar.
