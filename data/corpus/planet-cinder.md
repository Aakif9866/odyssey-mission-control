# Planet Profile: Cinder

Cinder is a tidally heated volcanic world orbiting close to its star. Dayside surface temperature averages 410 °C. Cinder is designated **robotic-only**; crewed landings are prohibited.

## Geology
Intense tidal heating drives constant volcanism. A network of lava tubes called the **Ashfall Tubes** runs beneath the southern plains. Tube roofs are unstable and collapse without warning.

## Missions
ODY-01 Firstlight (2203) was a crewed orbital mission and the first HED mission ever flown. ODY-04 Ember (2239) is a robotic surface mission with two landers, Kiln-1 and Kiln-2.
