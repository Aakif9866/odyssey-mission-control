# RD-440 Regolith and Ice Drill: Operations Manual (v1)

## Specifications
- Maximum drilling depth: **40 m**
- Bit type: CB-9 carbide
- Maximum rotation: 180 rpm
- Rated operating temperature: down to -160 °C

## Warning codes
- **V-11 (vibration):** reduce rotation to 120 rpm and continue.
- **V-20 (overheat):** stop drilling for 10 minutes.
