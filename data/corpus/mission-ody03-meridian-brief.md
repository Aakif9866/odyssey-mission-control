# Mission Brief: ODY-03 Meridian

**Destination:** Kepler-Vex, Halvorsen Basin
**Launch:** 2240-03-14 | **Arrival:** 2240-11-02
**Crew:** 6 | **Budget:** 4.2 billion credits | **Status:** Active
**Risk class:** B

Meridian was originally scheduled for 2236 but was delayed four years by a funding freeze, which is why it launched after ODY-04 despite its lower number.

## Objectives
1. Drill three boreholes (BH-1, BH-2, BH-3) to study the upper ice shell using the RD-440 drill.
2. Deploy a four-station seismometer array (S1–S4) to probe ice-shell thickness.
3. Search meltwater samples for biosignatures.

## Command and approvals
Commander: Imani Okafor-Reyes. Any change that alters the mission risk class, adds new hardware deployments, or exceeds approved drilling limits requires approval from the HED Director of Exploration, Halima Sato.

## Base
Meridian Station consists of Hab modules A and B, a drill garage, and a science lab.
