# Habitat Life Support Manual (v3)

## Error codes
- **E-104 (O2 generator low output):** inspect the electrolysis cell; switch to O2 reserve tanks if output stays below 70%.
- **E-217 (CO2 scrubber saturation):** swap to backup cartridge LS-CART-B within 30 minutes. Cabin CO2 must stay below 0.5%.
- **E-309 (water reclamation fault):** switch to stored water; reserve supports 9 days for 6 crew.
- **E-412 (thermal loop pressure loss):** evacuate the module if loop pressure falls below 60 kPa.

## Consumables
Each crew member uses 0.84 kg of oxygen per day.
