# Meridian Station Mission Log, Sols 41–48

**Sol 41** (Brandt): Borehole BH-2 reached 32 m. RD-440 raised vibration warning V-11; rpm reduced to 120 per manual v1.

**Sol 42** (Watanabe): Detected an unexplained periodic seismic signal beneath Halvorsen Basin: frequency 0.37 Hz, repeating every 6.2 hours. Logged as anomaly **ANM-0042**.

**Sol 43** (Brandt): RD-440 bit fractured in BH-2 at 38 m depth (ice temperature -148 °C). Drilling halted. BH-3 not yet started.

**Sol 44** (Mendez-Hale): Error **E-217** in Hab module B. Swapped to backup cartridge LS-CART-B within 18 minutes. CO2 returned to nominal.

**Sol 45** (Raman): Complex nitrogen-bearing organic molecules found in a BH-1 meltwater sample from 22 m. This is NOT a confirmed biosignature; abiotic origin is possible.

**Sol 46**: Cdr. Okafor-Reyes fractured her wrist during EVA. Under HED-OPS-7, command transferred to Lt. Cdr. Tomas Varga.

**Sol 47**: HED issued RD-440 manual revision v2. Drilling limits changed (see manual).

**Sol 48** (Watanabe): ANM-0042 period shortened from 6.2 h to 5.9 h. Proposal to deploy additional seismometers drafted; awaiting Director approval.
